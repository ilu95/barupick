[Description]

"What should I wear today?" BaruPick solves your daily outfit dilemma with AI-powered color coordination.

BaruPick is a styling assistant app that analyzes color harmony to find the outfit combinations that look best on you.

# Get Outfit Recommendations
Pick one item you want to wear, and BaruPick suggests 30 outfits with color harmony scores. Save your favorites and revisit them anytime.

# Build Your Own Outfit
Dress up a mannequin with your own color picks. AI suggests colors that match your taste, then scores the result across 7 categories: color placement, proportion, harmony, color temperature, balance, personal color, and body type fit.

# Personal Color Diagnosis & Body Type Guide
Find your personal color through a quick quiz (8 questions), a daylight comparison test, or by choosing directly from 12 seasonal types. The body type guide shows you how to use color placement and styling to flatter your figure.

# My Wardrobe
Register the clothes you own, and BaruPick creates outfits from them. It also analyzes what you use most and suggests what to buy next.

# Weather-Based Outfits
Get outfit suggestions based on today's weather — temperature, wind chill, humidity, and wind all factored in.

# OOTD Log & Outfit Calendar
Snap a photo of what you wore. Tag the occasion (work, date, casual, interview, travel, workout), add your mood, or cover your face with stickers. Your log builds into a calendar so you can check when you last wore something.

# Community
Browse other people's outfits and show off yours. You can also share privately with friends only.
